# Çengel Kapışması kapak görseli

BiMola lobisindeki düz mavi kapak, oyunun yeşil-beyaz tahtasını temsil eden yeni bir illüstrasyonla değiştirildi.

- Üretim: yerleşik imagegen aracı.
- Kullanılan dosya: `public/games/cengel-kapismasi/cover.webp`.
- Boyut: 1200 × 633; 47.114 bayt.
- Eski `cover.svg` adresi, açık odaları kesmeden çalışan yerel sunucunun da aynı görseli gösterebilmesi için aynı resmi gömülü içerir. Yeni katalog WebP adresini kullanır.

## Üretim promptu

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape game cover image for BiMola browser multiplayer game Çengel Kapışması. Primary request: a beautifully crafted playful premium 3D illustration of an arrowword crossword board with tactile letter tiles, conveying friends competing to complete words. Scene: a soft fresh sage green background, bright ivory and pale pistachio arrowword board floating at a slight isometric angle, letter tiles flying into the board in one expressive curved trajectory. Board squares must include sage clue cells with small right/down arrow symbols, interspersed among cream answer cells, not a chessboard. A few chunky ivory tiles carry clearly recognizable Turkish letters K, E, L, İ, M, E. A tiny restrained burst of warm yellow sparkles where a tile lands conveys success. Subject prominently fills center and lower center with convincing crafted clay-like materials, subtle rounded bevels, coherent soft shadows and high visual quality. Composition: landscape 1.9:1 cover, strong readable silhouette at thumbnail size, camera close enough to see the board, essential content in middle 75% so cropping is safe. Top left must remain visually quiet for an existing category badge overlay; bottom right visually quiet for existing arrow overlay. Palette: fresh mint, ivory, forest green lettering with tiny warm gold accents, matching the actual game board. Lighting: lovely broad soft studio illumination and dimensional shadows. Constraints: no full words, no title, no UI panels, no logos, no watermark, no characters, no score widgets. Only isolated letter glyphs on tiles and arrow icons on clue cells. Avoid a flat vector look, excessive confetti, dark backdrop, visual clutter. Output a finished game illustration, not a website mockup.
